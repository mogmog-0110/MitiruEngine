Input glyphs (assets/glyphs/*.png)
==================================

Source:  drawn by tools/gen_input_glyphs.py in the MitiruEngine repository
         (shapes only; no third-party artwork is included)
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
         https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Letters on the glyphs are rendered with M PLUS Rounded 1c Bold
(SIL Open Font License 1.1, assets/fonts/OFL.txt). The OFL does not
restrict images rendered with the font.

The button names and shapes describe the controls of common gamepads so
players can find the right button. They are not logos and do not imply
endorsement by any controller maker.
